Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CodeAnvil Internships & Certificates

Is there any registration fee?

Registration and your Internship Offer Letter are 100% FREE. Only after you complete your tasks do you pay a minimal ₹99 fee for project evaluation, QR verification hosting, and verified certificate issuance.

How do I pay the ₹99 certificate fee?

After you submit your tasks, scan the UPI QR code (9839411035@ptsbi) with any UPI app such as PhonePe, Google Pay or Paytm, then enter the 12-digit UTR number. You can also upload a screenshot or send it on WhatsApp. Your certificate unlocks once CodeAnvil approves your tasks and payment.

What does the certificate include?

Our certificates include your exact internship duration, domain, Intern ID, and a scannable verification QR code that anyone can use to check it.

How long does approval take?

We review tasks and payments manually, so please allow a few working days. For urgent questions, use the WhatsApp button at the bottom of the page.

What if my tasks are rejected?

You will see a note from the reviewer on your dashboard. Fix the issues and submit again. There is no extra fee for re-evaluation.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Open Intern Login, enter your email and tap "Forgot password?". We will email you a link to set a new password.

Is the internship paid?

No. This is an unpaid, remote learning internship focused on building real projects for your portfolio.
